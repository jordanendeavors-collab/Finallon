Stagehand Training Guide

Welcome, Stagehand!

You are going to help set up the sound system for the Lion King JR show. There are 12 steps. Follow them in order. Each step has a picture that shows you exactly what to do.

THREE RULES

  • Go slow. Doing it right beats doing it fast.
  • Buddy up. Every step gets checked by your buddy before you move on.
  • If unsure, ASK. Mr. Wayne would rather answer a question than fix a problem.
1

Meet the gear

Before you plug anything in, know what each box does. You're going to see three big black boxes on the FOH (Front Of House) table. FOH is a fancy word for 'where the sound person sits.'

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Nothing yet — just look at the table

DO THIS

  1. Find the box with 4 tall sticks on top (antennas) and 8 little color screens. That's the Receiver. It listens to all the microphones.
  2. Find the box with LOTS of knobs and sliders. That's the Mackie mixer. It's where we control how loud each person is.
  3. Find the box with blue knobs and lights that say GAIN REDUCTION. That's the dbx. It stops the sound from getting too loud and hurting ears.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • You can point to all three boxes without touching them
  • You can say each box's name out loud

WATCH OUT

DON'T turn anything on yet! We power on in the right order at Step 11.

2

Get the cables ready

You're going to use TWO kinds of cables tonight. Learning the difference is the first sound-tech skill.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 5 short XLR cables (6 feet)
  • 5 long XLR cables (25 feet)
  • 2 extra-long XLR cables (25-50 feet, usually black)
  • 1 or 2 music cables (1/4-inch plugs on both ends)

DO THIS

  1. XLR cables have 3 round metal pins on one end and 3 holes on the other. They look like a tiny crown.
  2. 1/4-inch cables look like big headphone plugs. Smooth metal tip, no pins.
  3. Lay each cable out STRAIGHT on the floor. Do not leave them coiled while hooking up — it's way harder to find the end you need.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • All XLR cables are in one pile, all 1/4-inch in another
  • No cables are tangled

WATCH OUT

If a cable has bent or missing pins, SET IT ASIDE. Bent pins break the jack they plug into.

3

Plug the Receiver into the Mackie (Step 1 of 5)

Now we start making the gear talk to each other. The Receiver hears the microphones, and we need to feed what it hears into the mixer.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 1 short XLR cable
  • The Receiver (box with 4 antennas)
  • The Mackie mixer

DO THIS

  1. Look at the BACK of the Receiver. Find the hole labeled CH 1 OUT (or Channel 1 Output).
  2. Push one end of the XLR cable into it. It only goes one way — line up the pins.
  3. Push until you hear/feel a CLICK. If no click, push firmer.
  4. Take the OTHER end. Look at the Mackie. Find Channel 1 (far left side). Look at the bottom — you'll see a round XLR hole.
  5. Push the cable in until it CLICKS.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Both ends are fully pushed in (no silver showing)
  • Cable is not pulling tight — leave a little slack

WATCH OUT

If the cable won't go in, it's upside down. Rotate it 180° and try again.

4

Plug in Receiver Channels 2, 3, 4, and 5

Do the exact same thing as Step 3, four more times. Each cast microphone channel gets its own cable.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 4 more XLR cables (1 short, 3 long)
  • The Receiver and Mackie

DO THIS

  1. Short cable: Receiver CH 2 OUT → Mackie Channel 2 XLR
  2. Long cable: Receiver CH 3 OUT → Mackie Channel 3 XLR
  3. Long cable: Receiver CH 4 OUT → Mackie Channel 4 XLR
  4. Long cable: Receiver CH 5 OUT → Mackie Channel 5 XLR
  5. CLICK every cable in both ends.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Five cables total go from Receiver to Mackie
  • Each Mackie Channel number matches each Receiver Channel number (1 to 1, 2 to 2, etc.)
  • Receiver Ch 6, 7, 8 OUTs are EMPTY on purpose — those are for spare microphones

WATCH OUT

Don't cross the cables! Ch 1 to Ch 1, Ch 2 to Ch 2. If you swap them, the wrong actor's voice comes out of the wrong knob.

5

Plug the Mackie into the dbx

Now we make the Mackie's main sound go through the dbx before it reaches the speakers. The dbx is like a seatbelt for the sound — it keeps the loud parts safe.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 2 short XLR cables (6 feet each)
  • Mackie mixer
  • dbx 1066 (box with blue knobs)

DO THIS

  1. On the BACK of the Mackie, find the two XLR holes labeled MAIN OUT LEFT and MAIN OUT RIGHT.
  2. Take one short XLR cable: Mackie MAIN OUT LEFT → dbx Ch 1 AUDIO IN.
  3. Take the other short XLR cable: Mackie MAIN OUT RIGHT → dbx Ch 2 AUDIO IN.
  4. CLICK both ends of both cables.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • LEFT goes to dbx Channel 1, RIGHT goes to dbx Channel 2 — always this way
  • Both cables fully clicked in

WATCH OUT

The dbx has two sides: IN and OUT. Make sure you put the cables in the IN side (usually marked 'AUDIO IN'). If you put them in OUT, no sound will come out.

6

Plug the dbx into the speakers

This is where the sound finally travels out to the audience. These are the LONGEST cables — they stretch all the way across the gym.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 2 very long XLR cables (25 feet or longer)
  • dbx 1066
  • Both powered speakers (the big black boxes with grilles)

DO THIS

  1. Take one long XLR cable: dbx Ch 1 AUDIO OUT → LEFT speaker XLR IN (the speaker on the audience's left side).
  2. Take the other long XLR cable: dbx Ch 2 AUDIO OUT → RIGHT speaker XLR IN.
  3. Run the cables neatly along the floor. Where someone might step over them, put down gaffer tape (the fabric tape, not the shiny silver tape).

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Both speakers have one XLR cable going into them
  • No cables stretched tight — leave slack
  • No cables lying across a walkway without tape

WATCH OUT

DO NOT use duct tape on the gym floor. It leaves sticky goo and the custodians will be grumpy at Mr. Wayne.

7

Plug in the music (if we have music tonight)

Music comes from a phone, laptop, or little box that someone brings. It plugs into the mixer differently than the microphones.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 1 music cable (both ends are 1/4-inch plugs, or one end plugs into a phone)
  • The music device (phone/laptop)
  • The Mackie mixer

DO THIS

  1. Plug the music cable's device-end into the phone or laptop.
  2. Find the OTHER end of the cable — there should be TWO 1/4-inch plugs (one usually red/orange, one white).
  3. On the Mackie, go to Channel 13. Look at the TOP of the channel strip — you'll see a 1/4-inch hole labeled LINE. Push the LEFT (or red) plug in there.
  4. Push the RIGHT (or white) plug into Channel 14 LINE hole.
  5. Set the phone to airplane mode so texts don't interrupt the show.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Music cable is in Channel 13 AND Channel 14
  • Phone is in airplane mode

WATCH OUT

If there's no music tonight, SKIP this step. Leave Channel 13 and 14 empty.

8

Plug everything into power

We're almost done hooking up. Now connect all the power cords — but DON'T turn anything on yet.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • One big power strip (surge protector)
  • 4 power cables (Receiver, Mackie, dbx, and music device)
  • 2 speaker power cables (speakers plug into wall)

DO THIS

  1. Plug the power strip into the wall. Leave the strip's switch OFF.
  2. Plug the Receiver's power cable into the strip.
  3. Plug the Mackie's power cable into the strip.
  4. Plug the dbx's power cable into the strip.
  5. Plug the music device's charger into the strip if it has one.
  6. Walk to each speaker. Plug its power cable into a wall outlet near the speaker. Leave each speaker switched OFF.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Power strip is in the wall but switch is OFF
  • All 4 boxes have their power cables plugged in
  • Both speakers have power to them but speaker switches are OFF

WATCH OUT

If anything turns on by accident, don't panic. Flip it back off and keep going.

9

Set the Mackie starting position

Before power-on, every knob and slider must start in the right spot. This is called 'zeroing the board.' We do this so the first sound doesn't BLAST the audience.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Just the Mackie — your hands

DO THIS

  1. ALL faders (the sliders at the bottom): slide them all the way DOWN.
  2. ALL TRIM knobs (top row): turn all the way to the LEFT (counter-clockwise).
  3. ALL PAN knobs: turn until you feel a little CLICK in the middle. That's 'center detent.'
  4. ALL MUTE buttons on Channels 1-5 and Channels 13-14: push them IN so the light comes on.
  5. ASSIGN 1-2 buttons on Channels 1-5: push IN (this sends cast voices to the Cast Bus).
  6. LOW CUT (75 Hz) buttons on Channels 1-5: push IN.
  7. MAIN L and MAIN R faders (far right): slide them all the way DOWN.
  8. GROUP 1 fader: all the way DOWN.

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • Every slider is at the very bottom
  • Every PAN knob is centered (you felt the click)
  • Red lights are ON for Channels 1-5 and 13-14 MUTE buttons

WATCH OUT

Don't touch anything on the BACK of the Mackie. Especially don't flip the +48V PHANTOM switch. That stays OFF.

10

Set the dbx starting position

The dbx needs its knobs in a specific spot too. Match the picture below.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Just the dbx — your hands

DO THIS

  1. Find the STEREO COUPLE button in the middle. Push it IN (light on).
  2. Find the BYPASS button on BOTH sides. Make sure both are OUT (light OFF).
  3. EXPANDER/GATE THRESHOLD knob: point to -40
  4. COMPRESSOR THRESHOLD knob: point to -12
  5. RATIO knob: point to 3:1
  6. OverEasy button: push IN (light on)
  7. ATTACK and RELEASE knobs: both to AUTO
  8. OUTPUT GAIN: between +3 and +6
  9. PeakStopPlus LIMITER: point to +6
  10. Do the above for BOTH Channel 1 and Channel 2 (same settings on both).

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • STEREO COUPLE light is ON
  • Both BYPASS lights are OFF
  • All knobs on Channel 1 match all knobs on Channel 2

WATCH OUT

On the BACK of the dbx, there's a switch for +4 or -10. Make sure it's set to +4.

11

POWER ON — in this exact order!

This is the most important part. If you turn things on in the wrong order, the speakers go BOOM and that's bad. Follow this order EXACTLY.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • The power strip
  • Your finger

DO THIS

  1. Flip the power strip's switch to ON. (Nothing should turn on by itself yet.)
  2. Wait 3 seconds.
  3. Turn ON the Receiver. Check that all 8 little screens light up and show numbers (frequencies).
  4. Wait 3 seconds.
  5. Turn ON the music device (if you have one).
  6. Wait 3 seconds.
  7. Turn ON the Mackie. Watch the lights on the right side flicker on.
  8. Wait 3 seconds.
  9. Turn ON the dbx. The green and red lights will do a quick dance.
  10. Wait 3 seconds.
  11. Walk to each speaker and turn them ON — LAST!

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • All 8 channels show frequencies on the Receiver
  • Mackie power light is on
  • dbx power light is on
  • Both speakers show a power light

WATCH OUT

If you hear a LOUD POP when a speaker turns on, STOP. That means something was powered on in the wrong order. Go get Mr. Wayne.

12

Final check — go get Mr. Wayne

You did it! Now Mr. Wayne needs to check your work before cast walks in. Stand back and look at the whole setup.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Your eyes
  • Mr. Wayne

DO THIS

  1. Walk around the FOH table. Make sure no cables are tangled or stepped-on.
  2. Count the cables from Receiver to Mackie: should be 5.
  3. Count the cables from Mackie to dbx: should be 2.
  4. Count the cables from dbx to speakers: should be 2.
  5. Look at the Mackie. All faders should be DOWN. All PAN centered.
  6. Look at the dbx. STEREO COUPLE should be ON. BYPASS should be OFF.
  7. Go find Mr. Wayne and say: 'I'm ready for check!'

YOU DID IT RIGHT WHEN...

  • All cables counted and correct
  • Mackie faders all down
  • dbx looks right
  • Mr. Wayne gave you a thumbs-up

WATCH OUT

If anything looks weird or you forgot a step, tell Mr. Wayne BEFORE he turns up the sound. Mistakes are okay. Hiding them is not.

ALL 12 STEPS DONE

You're officially a trained stagehand. Shake Mr. Wayne's hand. You earned it.
